Cookies on this website

Last updated: November 2026

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. This page explains how this website uses them.

Cookies we use

This website does not use any advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. We do not use cookies to identify you or follow you around the internet.

Fonts

The text on this website uses fonts supplied by Google Fonts. To show them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which means Google receives your device's IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies. You can read more in Google's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as the Information Commissioner's Office. Those websites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which we do not control.

If this changes

If we add analytics or any other cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will update this page and ask for your permission before setting them.

Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. The help pages for your browser will explain how.

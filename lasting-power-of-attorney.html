Lasting Powers of Attorney

A Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) lets you choose the people who can make decisions for you if illness or an accident means you can no longer make them yourself. Without one, your family may have to apply to the Court of Protection, which can be slow and costly.

There are two types, and many people choose both:

  • Property and Financial Affairs covers paying bills, managing bank accounts and selling property
  • Health and Welfare covers medical treatment, care and where you live

An LPA can only be made while you still have mental capacity, and it must be registered with the Office of the Public Guardian before it can be used. We handle the paperwork and registration for you.

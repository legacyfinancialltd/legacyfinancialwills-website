Privacy Policy

How we look after your information

Last updated: November 2026

Writing a will or a Lasting Power of Attorney means telling us personal things about you and your family. This policy explains what we collect, why we need it, who we share it with and the rights you have.

Who we are

Legacy Financial Estate Planning Ltd is the data controller for the personal information we hold about you. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 13833299, at Unit 4, Building 4, Innovation Way, Barnsley, S75 1JL. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number [ICO registration number].

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email hello@legacyfinancialwills.co.uk, call 07792 883425 or write to us at the address above.

What information we collect

  • Your name, address, date of birth, phone number and email address
  • Details of your family, such as your partner, children, executors, guardians, attorneys and beneficiaries
  • Details of your assets, such as your home, savings, pensions and life insurance
  • Your wishes for your estate, your care and your funeral
  • Proof of identity and address
  • Notes of our meetings, calls and emails with you

Sometimes we need more sensitive information, such as details about your health. This can be relevant to a Lasting Power of Attorney or a trust for someone vulnerable. We only collect this where it is needed for your documents, and we will ask for your explicit consent first.

When you give us information about other people, such as your children or the people named in your will, please let them know you have shared it with us.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it where needed to provide your service, including with:

  • Our legal partner, who may draft or check your documents. We will always tell you before we refer you, including any fee we receive for the introduction
  • The Office of the Public Guardian, when registering a Lasting Power of Attorney
  • Will storage providers, if you ask us to arrange storage
  • Legacy Financial Ltd, our sister company, only if you ask for mortgage or protection advice
  • Our IT, email and secure document providers, who process information on our behalf under contract
  • Regulators, courts or the police, where the law requires it

We aim to keep your information within the UK. If any provider processes it outside the UK, we make sure suitable safeguards are in place.

How long we keep it

We keep a record of your instructions and documents for as long as your will or Lasting Power of Attorney may be needed, because your family or executors may need to rely on them in the future. Other records, such as general enquiries that did not go ahead, are deleted after 2 years. Information kept for legal or tax reasons is held for at least 6 years.

How we keep it safe

We use secure systems, password protection and restricted access so that only the people who need your information can see it. Paper documents are kept securely in our office.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we no longer need to keep it
  • Ask us to limit how we use your information, or object to us using it
  • Ask us to transfer your information to another organisation
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will respond within one month and there is normally no charge.

Complaints about your information

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

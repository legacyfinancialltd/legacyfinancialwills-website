Complaints

Our complaints procedure

Last updated: November 2026

We work hard to get things right, but if you are unhappy with our service we want to know. Telling us gives us the chance to put things right and improve.

How to complain

You can complain in whichever way suits you:

  • Phone: 07792 883425
  • Email: hello@legacyfinancialwills.co.uk
  • Post: Complaints, Legacy Financial Estate Planning Ltd, Unit 4, Building 4, Innovation Way, Barnsley, S75 1JL

Please tell us your name and contact details, what went wrong, and what you would like us to do to put it right.

What happens next

  • We will acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and tell you who is dealing with it
  • We will look into it fully and may contact you for more information
  • We will send you our full written response within 4 weeks
  • If we need longer, we will write to explain why and when you can expect our answer, which will be no later than 8 weeks from when you first complained

If your complaint is about our legal partner

If your documents were prepared by our legal partner and your complaint is about their work, you can still contact us and we will pass it on and help you get an answer. Our legal partner also has its own complaints procedure, which we can send you.

If you are still unhappy

Will writing is not a regulated activity in England and Wales, so there is no ombudsman that covers our own service. If you are not happy with our final response, you can get free, independent advice from Citizens Advice.

If your complaint is about how we have used your personal information, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Mortgage and protection advice

If your complaint is about mortgage or protection advice from our sister company, Legacy Financial Ltd, a separate complaints procedure applies. Please contact us and we will send you the details.

Legacy Financial Estate Planning is part of Legacy Financial, an independent mortgage and protection firm based at Innovation Way in Barnsley.

Our story

We take your wishes seriously. Ourselves? Not so much.

For over seven years our owners have helped local families buy homes and protect their incomes. Again and again, clients asked us the same question: "What happens to all this when I'm gone?" Legacy Financial Estate Planning is our answer.

We are a young, owner run business and we do things differently. No jargon, no pressure and no hidden costs. Just clear advice from people who live and work in the same community as you.

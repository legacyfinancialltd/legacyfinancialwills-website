Why it matters

Without a will, the law decides who gets what.

If you die without a will in England and Wales, your estate is shared out under the rules of intestacy. Those rules follow a fixed order of family members and take no account of what you would have wanted.

  • Unmarried partners have no automatic right to inherit, however long you have lived together.
  • A will lets you name guardians for children under 18, so the people you trust raise them.
  • A Lasting Power of Attorney means the people you choose can help if you become unwell.
